Meet Our Founder

Rajput Devdhar Singh is a BTech Computer Engineering student at Indus University with a deep passion for web development. He started his coding journey with a simple mission: to build tools that solve real problems for people.

The idea for ToolTari came when he realized that most online tools either require login, cost money, or compromise user privacy. He wanted to create a platform where anyone can edit PDFs and images instantly without uploading files to any server.

Today, ToolTari serves thousands of users with 40+ free tools that work completely in your browser. He is committed to expanding this platform and helping users worldwide with fast, secure, and accessible tools.
